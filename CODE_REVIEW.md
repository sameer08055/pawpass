# Code Review – PawPass

**Reviewer:** Sameer

## First Impressions
The tagline "Pet care, handed off right" is catchy, and I understood what the site was about right away. Splitting the homepage into "I'm a Pet Owner" and "I'm a Sitter" makes it clear where to click.

## Pet Owner Side
- The **Quick Facts** link is my favorite feature. Sending a sitter one link with feeding, allergies, meds, and vet info is a smart idea, and making it read-only was a nice touch.
- The forms ask for the information a sitter would actually need.
- The health record types (vaccines, vet visits, weight, medication) feel practical.

## Sitter Side
- Custom time ranges on the availability calendar are a thoughtful detail.
- Setting different rates per animal is useful.
- The "what should owners prepare?" field shows you thought about the real-world handoff.

## Polish & UX
- Empty-state messages like "Add a pet on the My Pets page first" are helpful and guide the user.
- The dashboard, customers list, and map make it feel like a real app.

## Overall
Great job! You clearly put a lot of thought into this project.
